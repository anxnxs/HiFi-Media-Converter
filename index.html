Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

HiFi Media Converter ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application ("App") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use HiFi Media Converter, including information processed by third-party advertising services.

1. Information We Collect

Media Files

HiFi Media Converter allows users to convert audio and video files stored on their devices.

All media processing and conversion operations are performed locally on your device.

We do not upload, transmit, or store your media files on our servers. We do not access media files unless you explicitly select them for processing or grant the necessary access.

Device and Diagnostic Information

To improve application stability, performance, and reliability, we or our third-party service providers may collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash reports
  • Performance and diagnostic information

This information is used to identify technical issues and improve the App.

Advertising Information

HiFi Media Converter integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements.

When you use the App, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location information derived from your IP address
  • Advertising and app interaction data
  • Advertisement impressions and clicks
  • Device information, such as device model and operating system
  • Diagnostic and performance information related to advertising services

This information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Delivering and displaying advertisements
  • Personalizing advertisements based on relevant information and user interests, where permitted
  • Measuring advertising performance and effectiveness
  • Limiting repeated advertisements
  • Detecting and preventing invalid activity, fraud, and abuse

Depending on your location, consent status, and applicable privacy settings, you may receive personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

2. How We Use Information

We use information processed by the App and its third-party service providers for the following purposes:

  • Providing audio and video conversion functionality
  • Maintaining and improving application performance and stability
  • Diagnosing crashes and technical issues
  • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Measuring advertisement performance
  • Preventing advertising fraud and abuse
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your media files for advertising purposes.

3. Permissions

Depending on your device and Android version, the App may request certain permissions, including:

Storage and Media Access

Used solely to:

  • Read audio and video files selected by the user
  • Save converted files to the device
  • Access media files when explicitly requested by the user

The App does not access files that you have not selected or otherwise authorized.

Other Permissions

Certain technical permissions may be required to support application functionality, advertising services, and performance monitoring.

Permissions can be managed through your Android device settings.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App.

Because media conversion takes place locally on your device, your audio and video files remain under your control and are not transmitted to our servers.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

HiFi Media Converter is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Our advertising services may apply additional restrictions and safeguards where required by applicable laws and regulations.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have certain rights and choices regarding your information.

You may:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID through Android privacy settings, where supported.
